# Bannerlords of Westrun — Lore Collaboration

**[▶ Play the game](https://glennvanhaute-collab.github.io/turnbasegame/)**

Thanks for helping build the world of Westrun. No coding knowledge needed. Your job is the writing — we handle turning it into the game.

---

## How it works

1. **Pick something to write** — a hero, a raid, a house, a codex entry
2. **Copy the right template** from the `templates/` folder
3. **Fill it in** — write as much or as little as you want, notes are welcome
4. **Drop it in the right folder** (or send it directly to Glenn)
5. **It goes in the game** — Glenn or Claude drops it in on the next session

You don't need to worry about formatting, code, or matching anything exactly. If the vibe is right, we'll handle the rest.

---

## Folders

| Folder | What goes here |
|---|---|
| `heroes/` | Character lore, quotes, backstory for recruitable heroes |
| `raids/` | Full raid encounters — setting, phases, abilities, boss quotes |
| `codex/` | House lore, faction history, named locations, story fragments |
| `templates/` | Blank templates to copy and fill in |

## The world so far

**Westrun** is a world of old houses, forgotten wars, and gear that remembers what it survived.

You play as a hedge knight — nobody, with nothing. You build a camp, forge connections, and earn the right to tell the stories of the people who join you.

### Tone

Think: low-fantasy, character-driven, earned. Not grimdark for its own sake. Heroes have reasons. Villains had them too.

The LOTR principle applies: Sting wasn't special because of its stats. It was special because of what it survived. Write characters the same way.

## Tips for writing heroes

- **One good quote beats three okay ones.** The quote appears right under their portrait. It should sound like them.
- **Lore is 2–4 sentences, third person.** It's a biography, not a story. What defines them? What do people say about them?
- **You don't need to invent their stats or skills** — that's on us. Focus on who they are.
- **Notes are welcome.** If you have ideas about how they fight, who they know, what they'd never do — write it in a Notes section. We read it.
