## Lore

**Biography (2–4 sentences, third person):**
(What defines them? What's the one thing people know about them? What would surprise you?)

> Example: *"Her library is the largest in Caelwyn. Visitors assume it is filled with ancient wisdom and forgotten spells — and they are not entirely wrong. But most of the shelves hold something more dangerous: meticulous records of every person she has ever met. What they promised. What they did instead. What they owe."*
