# Codex Template

Codex entries are the lore layer of Westrun — history, house records, named places, story fragments, and bonds between characters.

They're discovered in-game through dungeons, exploration, and companionship. The player finds them, not reads them upfront.

Copy this file, rename it, and fill it in.

---

## Entry Type

Pick one:

- [ ] **House history** — the founding, the wars, the secrets
- [ ] **Named location** — a place in Westrun worth remembering
- [ ] **Story fragment** — a scene, a letter, a recovered journal page
- [ ] **Companionship bond** — a connection between two heroes
- [ ] **Character deep lore** — extended backstory for a specific hero
- [ ] **Other:** ___________

## Content

Write the entry itself. It can be:
- Third-person history ("House Caelwyn was founded before the other houses existed...")
- First-person fragments ("Day 12. The king's men came again today...")
- Overheard dialogue
- A recovered letter
- A myth or legend passed down in the world

Length: anything from a paragraph to a full page. Longer is fine — this is lore, players who want it will read it.
