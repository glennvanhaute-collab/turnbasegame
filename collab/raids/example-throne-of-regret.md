This is the first completed raid, shown as an example.

---

## Overview

**Raid Name:** The Throne of Regret
**Boss Name:** Batman, Lord of Nightmares
**Subtitle:** Dungeon Arena
**Difficulty:** 5/5
**Setting:** A ruined throne room suspended in an endless void.

---

## Arena Features

- Endless darkness beyond the walls
- Thousands of empty thrones
- Ghostly citizens watching silently
- Giant stained-glass windows depicting happier times
- Swarms of shadow bats circling overhead
- A massive broken crown hanging above the arena

---

## Phase 1 — The Noble King

**Starts at:** Full HP

**What's happening:** Batman still believes he can save everyone.

| Ability | Description |
|---|---|
| Royal Decree | Marks players with impossible expectations. Damage increases based on how many allies are alive. |
| Burden of Duty | Absorbs damage intended for his minions. |
| "I Can Fix This" | Heals himself whenever players remove debuffs. |

---

## Phase 2 — Prisoner of the Mind

**Triggers at:** 70% HP

**Transition:** The throne room collapses.

**What's happening:** The king begins arguing with voices nobody else can hear. His glasses crack. One eye glows bright gold. The other burns crimson.

| Ability | Description |
|---|---|
| Endless Doubt | Creates shadow clones of the players. |
| What If I Failed? | Forces players to relive previous mistakes. |
| The Labyrinth | The arena becomes a maze of memories. |

---

## Phase 3 — Batman, Lord of Nightmares

**Triggers at:** 30% HP

**Transition:** The crown shatters.

**What's happening:** The king realizes he can never save everyone. Instead of accepting it — he embraces the darkness. His beard becomes streaked with shadow. His glasses become a black void. Great wings made of living darkness erupt from his back.

| Ability | Description |
|---|---|
| Kingdom of One | Instantly destroys all summoned creatures. |
| Prison of the Mind | Traps players in their own fears. |
| The Endless Night | Arena slowly darkens until only his glowing silhouette remains. |
